# Episcopal Church of the Holy Apostles — Hilo, Hawaiʻi

> *"A place where friends become family. All are welcome! No exceptions."*

A luxury, high-end, soft white website redesign for the **Episcopal Church of the Holy Apostles** in Hilo, Hawaiʻi. Built with zero dependencies using vanilla semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 (with custom properties, gentle feathered shadows, and soft glassmorphism), and vanilla JavaScript.

---

## 🌺 Overview & Authentic Heritage

- **Parish Name:** Episcopal Church of the Holy Apostles
- **Location:** 1407 Kapiʻolani Street, Hilo, HI 96720 *(across from the University of Hawaiʻi at Hilo)*
- **Diocese:** Episcopal Diocese of Hawaiʻi
- **Founded:** 1903 (celebrating over 123 years of ministry in Hilo)
- **Official Links:**
  - [Official Parish Website](https://www.echa-hilo.org/)
  - [Official Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/churchoftheholyapostleshilo/)

## ✨ Features

- **Soft & Serene Design System:**
  - Gentle on the eyes with clean white (`#FFFFFF`), warm alabaster (`#FAF9F6`), and soft linen ivory (`#F4F1EA`).
  - Refined liturgical crimson (`#7C1827`) and warm champagne gold (`#B8860B`, `#C89B3C`) accents.
  - Classical serif typography ([Cinzel](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel) & [Cormorant Garamond](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cormorant+Garamond)) paired with [Plus Jakarta Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans).
- **Full-Width Hero Slider:**
  - Autoplaying banner with Ken Burns zoom-in effect.
  - Floating left social rail with tooltip badges.
  - Right-side vertical pagination indicator.
  - Floating 3-card quick dock for Sunday worship, outreach, and weekly gatherings.
- **100% Authentic Imagery:**
  - Every image is an authentic photograph from `echa-hilo.org` and the church's Facebook page. Zero stock images.
- **Interactive Worship & Sacraments:**
  - Tabbed schedule switcher for Regular Sundays (8:00 AM Contemplative & 10:00 AM Choral with Choir), 3rd Sunday Combined (9:00 AM with Lanai Potluck), and Weekday fellowships.
- **Sacred Labyrinth Feature:**
  - Guide to the unicursal prayer path open daily to the public on church grounds.
- **Ministries & Community Outreach:**
  - Interactive filterable cards (Peanut Butter Ministry, Lanai Gatherings, Kupuna Support, Diocesan Leadership, Lanai Zumba & recovery groups).
- **Stewardship & Offering:**
  - Interactive giving selector for General Operating, Peanut Butter Outreach, Labyrinth Preservation, and Music Fund.
- **Fully Responsive:**
  - Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices with a slide-out drawer menu.

## 🚀 Getting Started

No build step or Node.js runtime required. Simply clone the repository and open `index.html` in any web browser:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/jacobdesignixer-cmyk/holy-apostles-church.git
cd holy-apostles-church
# Open index.html in your browser
```
